# Style notes

Rules from the owner's calibration reviews (spec 10 §1, §6). Every writer and reviewer brief includes this file.

## A1 calibration (unit 1, 26 Nov 2026)

The owner reviewed unit 1 and had **no corrections**. The choices made in unit 1 therefore stand as the house style:

1. **Italics are Polish only.** English words in sound hints or glosses are plain text ("sh" in shop), never italic.
2. **Chunks before grammar.** A page may use a fixed phrase before its grammar is taught (*w domu*, *z Polski*, *Mam … lat*, *w sobotę*, *w lipcu*), always glossed and never tested as a rule before its topic. Forward links point to the topic that teaches it.
3. **Sets.** Only `verbs`, `grammar` and `cases` topics get an exercise set (10 items). `functions` and `vocabulary` topics have a page and a quiz.
4. **Pronunciation and stress** are shown with hyphens and capitals in Polish (italic) text: *war-SZA-wa*. Multiple-choice options never differ only in capital letters (the validator treats them as duplicates); ask for the syllable instead.
5. **Typed answers.** Accept the subject-pronoun variant, common word-order variants, and common informal equivalents a teacher would accept (*Co u pani słychać?* for "How are you?").
6. **Dialogues:** neighbours and colleagues may be *pan Adam*, *pani Ewa*; friends use short name forms (*Ania*, *Piotrek*); nominative instead of vocative in greetings is fine at A1 (*Cześć, Ania!*).
