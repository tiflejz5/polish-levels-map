# Instrumental after być and with z

In English "I am a student" uses the same form of "student" as "the student is here". In Polish a **job, nationality or role** after a form of *być* changes its ending: *Jestem studentem.* *Ona jest lekarką.* This form is the **instrumental** (*narzędnik*, questions: *kim? czym?*). The same case follows *z* when it means "with": *kawa z mlekiem* (coffee with milk). Adjectives alone after *być* do **not** change: *Jestem zmęczony.*

## How it works

Singular. The plural comes at A2.

| Gender | Noun | Adjective | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| masculine | **-em** (after *k, g*: **-iem**) | **-ym** (after *k, g*: **-im**) | *student → studentem*, *Polak → Polakiem*, *dobry lekarz → dobrym lekarzem* |
| feminine | **-a → -ą** | **-a → -ą** | *studentka → studentką*, *dobra lekarka → dobrą lekarką* |
| neuter | **-em** (after *k, g*: **-iem**) | **-ym** (after *k, g*: **-im**) | *mleko → mlekiem*, *małe dziecko → małym dzieckiem* |

Men's words ending in *-a* take *-ą* like feminine nouns: *tata → tatą*, *kolega → kolegą*.

## When to use it

1. **Job, nationality or role after *być*:** *Jestem nauczycielką.* (I'm a teacher. — a woman speaking) *Marek jest Polakiem.* (Marek is Polish.)
2. **With an adjective + noun:** *To jest dobry lekarz.* but *On jest dobrym lekarzem.* (He's a good doctor.)
3. ***z* meaning "with":** *herbata z cytryną* (tea with lemon), *kanapka z serem* (a cheese sandwich), *Ewa z mężem* (Ewa and her husband; *mąż → mężem*).
4. **Not after *to jest*:** *to jest* + nominative. *To jest student.* (This is a student.) but *On jest studentem.* (He's a student.)
5. **Not for an adjective alone:** *Jestem zmęczona.* (I'm tired. — a woman speaking)

## Examples

| Polish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Jestem studentem, a moja siostra jest lekarką. | I'm a student, and my sister is a doctor. |
| Kim jesteś z zawodu? — Jestem kelnerką. | What do you do? — I'm a waitress. |
| Pan Nowak jest Polakiem, a pani Smith jest Angielką. | Mr Nowak is Polish, and Ms Smith is English. |
| Mój tata jest bardzo dobrym kucharzem. | My dad is a very good cook. |
| Kawa z mlekiem, proszę. | A coffee with milk, please. |
| Herbata z cytryną czy z miodem? | Tea with lemon or with honey? |
| To jest Kasia z bratem. | This is Kasia with her brother. |
| On jest wysoki i bardzo miły. | He's tall and very nice. |

## Good to know

- *ze* instead of *z* before some clusters that start with *s, z, ś*: *ze śmietaną* (with cream), *ze Zbyszkiem* (with Zbyszek).
- *z* + a different case means "from": *Jestem z Polski.* That is the genitive: [[a1-cases-genitive-basics]].
- Nationality nouns take a capital letter: *Jestem Kanadyjczykiem / Kanadyjką.*
- Your **name** stays in the nominative: *Jestem Tom.* (never *Tomem*)
- Women may also use the masculine job title: *Ona jest dobrym lekarzem.*

## Common mistakes for English speakers

- ✗ *Jestem student.* → ✓ *Jestem studentem.* — a job after *być* takes the instrumental.
- ✗ *Ona jest lekarka.* → ✓ *Ona jest lekarką.* — feminine nouns change *-a* to *-ą*.
- ✗ *Jestem zmęczonym.* → ✓ *Jestem zmęczony.* — an adjective alone stays in the nominative.
- ✗ *kawa z mleko* → ✓ *kawa z mlekiem* — *z* meaning "with" takes the instrumental.
- ✗ *To jest studentem.* → ✓ *To jest student.* — after *to jest* the noun does not change.
